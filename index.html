SCIENTIFIC FOUNDATION

What is Morphometric Analysis?

Morphometric analysis is the quantitative measurement and mathematical evaluation of the Earth's surface — the shape, dimension and configuration of landforms and drainage networks. By deriving indices from elevation models and stream networks, it converts terrain into numbers that reveal how a river basin stores, routes and releases water and sediment.

⛰️

RELIEF ASPECT

Absolute Relief, Relative Relief, Dissection Index and Ruggedness quantify vertical terrain energy — the driver of erosion intensity and runoff velocity.

🌊

DRAINAGE ASPECT

Drainage Density and Stream Frequency describe how finely a landscape is carved by channels — a proxy for permeability, vegetation and runoff potential.

💧

HYDROLOGIC INDICES

TWI, SPI and STI model wetness accumulation, stream power and sediment transport capacity — core inputs for flood and soil-loss assessment.
